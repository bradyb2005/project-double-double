# **2\. Team Agreement Report**

### **Team Values**

- Timely responsiveness  
- Honesty and Trust  
- Constructive Criticism  
- Respect  
- Equal distribution  
- Task deadlines:  
  - We aim to complete tasks at least 2 days before the deadline, so that if something goes wrong we have leeway and don’t need to panic.

&nbsp;

### **Meetings**

- We agree to check in weekly by meeting in person or online. In person is preferred.  
- In these meetings, mental checkins and task progress \- evaluate

&nbsp;

### **Communication**

- We agree to use iMessages as our main communication channel \- We already have a group chat set up.  
- GitHub Projects for task board

&nbsp;

### **Response expectations**

- We agree to respond quickly to messages from the group chat, with a 24 hour turnaround unless otherwise agreed upon. When deadlines are approaching, this changes to a 12 hour turnaround time.  
- Respond with kindness and empathy

&nbsp;

### **Respect and Professional Conduct**

- Everyone is expected to be treated fairly, and everyone should have equal say in what we do as a group. No one should feel unheard or overpowered.  
- If you see disrespect, acknowledge it, don’t ignore it

&nbsp;

### **Accountability**&nbsp;

- We all agree that in a situation where a group member is disrespectful, we will attempt to resolve the issue ourselves before contacting COSC 310 staff.  
- In the event that someone in our group is disrespectful:  
  - First, talk to the person individually and create a plan to resolve it. If the action is still persisting;  
  - Second, talk to the group with all members included to discuss the issue and possible resolutions.  
  - If the person is still being disrespectful after both: contact COSC 310 staff

&nbsp;

### **Decision-making**

- When making a decision, brainstorm possible options, weigh the pros and cons, and we aim to first unanimously decide on something.  
- If we cannot decide on something, we bring it to a vote.  
  - In the event of a tie, we will further discuss until this tie is broken.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

### **Conflict resolution**

- If someone feels there is an issue with anything, bring it up immediately.  
- Bring the issue up within 7 days maximum, after that you can no longer bring it up.  
- Any minor issues, such as someone saying something offensive, should be resolved from within the group.  
- Disagreements should be resolved with a level head, a clear idea of what the problem is and a plan of how to resolve it.&nbsp;  
- If a conflict starts to create a high level of emotion from one or more parties, a break is appropriate and they can schedule a time to revisit the conversation at a later time.

&nbsp;

### **Anticipated challenges**

- The most common challenge in these kinds of group projects is when someone doesn’t complete a task on time or the way that they were supposed to. However, if treated correctly, this doesn’t even have to be a conflict. If I make clear my mindset of not blaming or shaming people for not completing things and supporting each other in these cases, then there is a good chance something like this won’t even be considered a conflict. If a struggling group member can A, be honest, and B, be willing to receive help, and the rest of the group can be supportive without retaliating, then there will be no conflict.  
- The team availability can be resolved by prioritizing a time to work on the project, even if that means it is a weekend

&nbsp;
